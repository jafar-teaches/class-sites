    Where this model simplifies
    • The pump's resistance here never changes, at 2.4 Ω. A real pump motor behaves in a more complicated way.
    • Pump speed here is just the voltage left at the pump compared with 12.0 V. A real pump's speed depends on more than this.
    • Kai's battery here always gives exactly 12.0 V. A real battery gives a little less when a big current flows.
    • Each wire's resistance stays the same here. In a real wire, resistance goes up a little as the wire warms up.
    • The numbers in each step are rounded, so the working uses the rounded values you can see.
