Habits that earn marks in any science test.

Read the command word

"State" or "identify" needs one short answer. "Explain" needs a reason, so use "because". "Evaluate" needs a judgement and a reason, using the data.

Set out every calculation

Write the formula, put in the numbers, then give the answer with a unit. Marks are given for each step, even if your final answer is wrong.

I = VR = 93 = 3 A

Change mA to A first

A formula needs amps, so change milliamps before you use it. Divide by 1,000, which moves the decimal point three places left.

150 mA = 0.15 A

Quote the data

When a question says "use the data", copy the actual numbers into your answer. "It went up from 0.2 A to 0.4 A" earns the mark; "it went up" does not.

Answer every multiple-choice question

Section A goes on the separate answer sheet. Wrong answers lose no marks, so never leave one blank. Work out your answer before you look at the options.

Pace yourself

You have a bit more than a minute per mark: about 10 minutes for Section A and 45 for Section B. If a part is taking too long, move on and come back to it.
